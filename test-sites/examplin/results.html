Important Safety Information

Indication

Examplin is a prescription maintenance inhaler for adults 18 and older with moderate-to-severe asthma. Examplin is not for treating sudden breathing problems.

Who should not use Examplin

Do not use Examplin if you are allergic to examplitide or any of the ingredients in Examplin.

Possible serious side effects

Serious side effects can include worsening breathing problems right after use, serious allergic reactions, and thrush in the mouth or throat. Get medical help right away if your breathing gets worse.
